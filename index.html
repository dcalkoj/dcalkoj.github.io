About Me!

Hello! I am Daniel Kalkoj, a recent graduate from the University of Michigan! I'm a big fan of software, hardware, software for hardware, and...well...I think you get the point (ф_ф*)

In my professional life, I have a passion for embedded systems and computer engineering projects. I enjoy working on projects that have a physical component - whether that's prototyping and working on human-centric systems, or reading datasheets and taking scope observations. For all that and more, I'm your guy!

In my personal life, I take things a bit slower. I'm a big fan of night drives, finding the perfect (educational, of course) YouTube video for my meal, and raiding thrift shops for usable e-waste! In the future, I think I'd like to get into hiking. Currently working on my treadmill endurance.
